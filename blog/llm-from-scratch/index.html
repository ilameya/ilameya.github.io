LLMs from Scratch

Build a small GPT in PyTorch, one piece at a time, and understand every line.

Large language models can feel like magic. They are not. Underneath, a model like GPT is a few hundred lines of code that do one thing: predict the next token. In this series we write those lines ourselves, train the result on Shakespeare on an ordinary laptop, and look inside at every step.

You need: Python, basic NumPy or PyTorch, and matrix multiplication. No GPU required.
Code: github.com/ilameya/llm-from-scratch, one runnable script per part.
